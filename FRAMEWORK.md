# EFMW-TLON-001 — Tlonification Framework
Version 0.1.0 — proposed, unvalidated.

## Definitions
Hyperstition: representation-induced intervention that contributes to realization of the represented outcome. Tlonification: adoption of a representational system displacing independently supported knowledge. Ontological lock-in: dependence that makes correction costly. Ontological Singularity: proposed persistent regime of growing unresolved claims, unsupported adoption, measured divergence and impaired correction.

## Seven modeling postulates
Representation; intervention; recursion; amplification; institutional adoption; independence of validity from adoption; correction by independent evidence.

## Dynamics
State (N,R,B,A,V,P): narrative, material state, belief, adoption, verification capacity, production capacity.
N'=F(N,R,B,P,E); R'=G(R,A,W); A'=H(N,B,C); B'=J(B,N,R,E).
E is independently acquired evidence; W exogenous influences; C constraints.
Local scalar loop gain H=(dA/dN)(dR/dA)(dN'/dR). Stability requires the full Jacobian, not H alone.
Backlog Q'=max(0,Q+g-v). Pressure g/v, undefined when v=0. Divergence is measured on held-out outcomes under a declared intervention policy. Distinguish causal fulfillment from prediction using randomized interventions or explicit causal assumptions.
Risk index L=(a+d+u+m)/4: normalized unsupported adoption, divergence, unresolved share, representational dependence. Equal weights are arbitrary. Do not infer truth from the index.

## Regimes
Imagination, exploration, hyperstition, discovery, Tlonification, lock-in, proposed ontological singularity. They need not be sequential or exclusive.
Singularity requires sustained backlog growth AND unsupported adoption AND independently measured divergence AND degraded correction, with thresholds fixed before assessment.

## Epistemic conservation
Rephrasing or multiplying a claim does not add independent empirical evidence. Deduction may add logical consequences without validating premises. Track sources as a dependency DAG and count independent evidence roots, not citations.

## Principal protocol
Record rationale before action, authorization, counterparty, evidence, independent reviewer, challenge route and reversal authority. Keep human sovereignty and consent. Distinguish imagination, feasibility, validity and authorization. Never treat self-attestation as independent assurance.

## Intellectual context
Borges, Tlön, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius (1940); CCRU hyperstition; self-fulfilling prophecy and causal inference. No novelty or priority claim established. The reported Altman post is unverified.
