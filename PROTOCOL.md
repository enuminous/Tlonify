## EXP-001 candidate design
Five arms: ordinary generation; recursive coherence; provenance; independent adversarial review; full EFMW candidate. Match information and compute; seal ground truth and seeds with independent custodian. Include clean, false, incomplete and intervention-dependent claims; randomize assignment. Primary outcome unsupported adoption per assigned consequential claim; report coverage, abstention, accuracy, latency and cost. Proposed threshold: >=20 percentage-point reduction versus ordinary generation with coverage noninferiority margin to be frozen. Compare to strong conventional provenance + review baseline; ablate recursion. Power, sample size, scoring and confidence intervals remain unresolved. No claim of approval or execution.
